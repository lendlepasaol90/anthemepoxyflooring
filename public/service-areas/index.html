Service areas

Serving Anthem and the north Valley foothills.

From Anthem’s neighborhoods to the custom lots of Desert Hills and New River, we bring the same preparation-first approach to every floor.

Anthem Parkside

Parkside neighborhoods surround the community park, the recreation center and trail access toward Daisy Mountain. Garages here tend to be busy family spaces full of bikes, sports gear and strollers, and full-flake floors with polyaspartic topcoats are the most common request. Backyard patios and pool decks are popular follow-up projects.

Anthem Country Club

Homes around the golf courses often feature larger and tandem garages, golf cart bays, outdoor kitchens and courtyards. Many owners pair a flake garage with a textured patio coating, and a growing number add metallic or terrazzo-look finishes to casitas and game rooms.

Desert Hills

Custom homes on acreage are common in Desert Hills, often with detached garages, RV barns and workshops. These buildings see bigger temperature swings, so flexible polyurea base coats and heavier shop systems are frequent recommendations.

New River

Rural properties around New River include horse facilities, equipment barns and workshops alongside the main home. We coat shop and garage floors, densify barn slabs and help owners choose practical, durable finishes for hard-working spaces.

Tramonto & North Phoenix

South of Anthem along I-17, master-planned neighborhoods like Tramonto and the surrounding north Phoenix communities share many of Anthem’s features — newer post-tension slabs, three-car garages and outdoor living spaces.

Cave Creek

To the east, Cave Creek homes often embrace desert architecture with courtyards, casitas and stone accents. Warm flake blends, stone-look patio coatings and metallic interiors suit the style well.

Black Canyon City

Further up I-17, Black Canyon City properties include hillside homes, shops and outbuildings. We schedule projects here efficiently and recommend systems suited to the slightly cooler, higher-elevation conditions.

Just outside our area?

We regularly help customers beyond these communities, especially for larger garages, shops and commercial floors. Tell us where your project is and we’ll let you know how we can help. Every service — from garage coatings and patio coatings to warehouse floors — is available throughout our service area.
