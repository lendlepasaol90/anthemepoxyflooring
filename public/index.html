Epoxy flooring in Anthem

Most Anthem homes have beautiful finishes everywhere — except the concrete. We fix that.

Walk through almost any home in Anthem and you’ll find thoughtful details: stone accents, tile roofs, outdoor kitchens, carefully planned landscaping. Then you open the door to the garage and find bare gray concrete with an oil spot or two, or a patio slab that has faded, cracked and collected years of stains. Those surfaces get used every day, yet they’re often the last to receive any attention.

A professionally installed floor coating closes that gap. It gives garages, patios and interior slabs a finished, durable surface that is easy to clean and holds up to real life — kids’ bikes, muddy trail shoes, the boat trailer, the weekend project and the steady drift of desert dust.

Our work is built on preparation. Every slab is diamond-ground to remove the weak top layer, old sealers and paint. Cracks and chips are repaired. Moisture is tested. Only then do we build the coating system, layer by layer, using materials chosen for the space: a flexible base where a slab moves, a decorative flake broadcast for texture, and a UV-stable topcoat wherever sunlight reaches.

The result is a floor that looks like it belongs in your home — and still looks that way years from now. Start with our garage floor coatings, or explore every system below.

Garage epoxy flooring

Installation process

From bare slab to finished floor.

Swipe or scroll through the steps. Most garages are complete in one to two days; interiors and large commercial floors take longer.

Step 1

Consult & test

We measure, inspect cracks and old coatings, check moisture and show you real samples. You get a written, itemized proposal.

Step 2

Grind

Diamond grinders with HEPA vacuums open the surface evenly. Edges and corners are finished with smaller tools.

Step 3

Repair & clean

Cracks and spalls are filled and ground flush. The floor is vacuumed spotless so nothing gets trapped under the coating.

Step 4

Base & design

The base coat goes down and the look is built — flake broadcast, solid pigment or metallic movement.

Step 5

Topcoat

A clear, sun-stable topcoat seals and protects. Optional grit adds traction where you need it.

Step 6

Walkthrough

We review the floor together and give you clear timing for foot traffic, parking and moving back in.

Local flooring considerations

Things we account for on every Anthem project.

Post-tension slabs

Much of Anthem was built from the late 1990s onward, and post-tension foundations are common. These slabs contain tensioned steel cables, so we never saw-cut, core or drill. Surface grinding is safe, and all of our standard prep stays at the surface.

Foothill sun and heat

Sitting a bit higher than central Phoenix doesn’t make summer gentle. Garage slabs heat up quickly and sunlight reaches deep through open doors. We schedule summer work early in the day and finish every sun-exposed floor with a UV-stable topcoat.

Monsoon dust and rain

Summer storms drive dust under garage doors and send water across patios. Mid-tone flake blends hide the dust, and textured finishes keep wet surfaces secure.

HOA guidelines

Interior and garage floors are typically your call, but visible surfaces like front entries and walkways may fall under neighborhood guidelines. We’re glad to provide samples and product information if you need approval.

Bigger garages, bigger plans

Three-car, tandem and RV-height garages are common. Larger floors need careful sequencing so the flake broadcast and topcoat look consistent from one end to the other.

What to expect from a quote

A visit that answers questions, not a sales pitch.

Our estimates happen at your home, because a floor can’t be priced properly from a photo. We start by walking the space with you and asking how it’s used. Is the garage mostly for parking, or is there a workbench and a treadmill too? Does the patio get afternoon sun? Is there a trailer that parks in the third bay? Last, full-size chip and color boards go right on your concrete so you can judge them under your own lights. You leave with an itemized written quote that lists the preparation, any repairs and each coating layer — so you can compare it fairly against anyone else’s.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

My home has a post-tension slab. Can it still be coated?

Yes. Many homes in Anthem were built on post-tension slabs, which contain tensioned steel cables. Coating is completely safe because we only grind the top surface — we never cut, core or drill into the slab. If a repair ever calls for anything deeper, we stop and discuss it with you first.

Does my HOA need to approve a floor coating?

Garage and interior floors generally aren’t visible from the street, so they rarely need approval. Front entries, walkways and driveways can be a different story. If the area is visible, check your neighborhood guidelines before choosing a color; we’re happy to provide samples or product information for your submission.

How long does a typical Anthem garage take?

Most two- and three-car garages take one to two days. With a fast-cure polyaspartic system, the whole floor can often be finished in a single day, with foot traffic the next morning and vehicles back within a few days.

Which finish hides dust best?

A full-broadcast flake floor in a mid-tone blend. Sand, tan and gray mixes disguise the fine desert dust that drifts in under garage doors far better than solid colors or very light and very dark blends.

Is an epoxy patio too hot to walk on in summer?

Color makes the biggest difference. Light, reflective shades stay far more comfortable than dark ones, and a textured finish keeps feet secure. We help you choose a color suited to your patio’s sun exposure.

Will my new floor turn yellow?

Standard epoxy can amber in sunlight, which is why we finish garages, patios and sunny rooms with a UV-stable polyaspartic or aliphatic urethane topcoat that keeps colors true.

Can you coat a floor that has old paint or a DIY kit on it?

Yes. We grind off old paint and failing coatings completely so the new system bonds to clean concrete. Coating over a weak layer is the most common reason floors fail.

Do you offer estimates outside Anthem?

We regularly work in Desert Hills, New River, Tramonto and the north Phoenix neighborhoods along I-17, as well as Cave Creek and Black Canyon City. Ask about your location.
