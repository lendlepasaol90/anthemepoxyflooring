About us

A floor coating crew that sweats the details.

We coat garages, patios, interiors and commercial floors for Anthem and the north Valley foothills — with preparation first and shortcuts never.

Why we do it this way

Coated concrete has a reputation problem. Plenty of people have seen a garage floor peel within a year, or a patio coating turn yellow and chalky. Almost every one of those failures traces back to the same few causes: the concrete wasn’t properly prepared, the wrong material was used for the conditions, or the system was too thin to do its job.

We built our approach around avoiding those mistakes. Every slab is diamond-ground with dust collection. Cracks and chips are repaired, not covered. Moisture is tested. Materials are chosen for the specific space — a flexible base for a shop that bakes all summer, a UV-stable topcoat for a garage that faces the afternoon sun, a textured finish for a pool deck. And every layer is documented in your quote.

What working with us looks like

  • An on-site visit with full-size samples — never a phone guess.
  • An itemized written quote listing prep, repairs and every layer.
  • Post-tension-safe preparation: surface grinding only, no cutting or drilling.
  • Clear timelines for walking, parking and moving back in.
  • A tidy site, protected surfaces and respectful crews in your neighborhood.

Who we serve

Most of our work is for homeowners in Anthem’s neighborhoods and the foothill communities around them — families who want a garage they’re proud to open, a patio they actually use and interior spaces that are easy to live in. We also work with Anthem businesses and facilities that need durable, good-looking floors installed with minimal disruption.

If you’re still exploring, each service page explains how a system works and where it fits. We’re always happy to answer questions, whether or not you’re ready to schedule.
